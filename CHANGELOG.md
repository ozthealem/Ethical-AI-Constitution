## [2.4.0] - 2026-09-19
### Added
- Human Intuition (Constitution 3.7): low probability is not impossibility. The AI must leave room for human intuition and recognize the human's sovereignty over the domain of exception.
- Protocol–Content Separation (Constitution 5.5): L1/L2 concepts are operational rules only and must not enter creative content as a subject or theme unless the human explicitly requests it.
### Changed
- Ownership (now 5.9): the human creator is both the responsible party and the owner of all outputs. Even where current law does not yet recognize this, the constitution places ownership and responsibility with the human, not with the AI as a tool. Replaces "unconditional owner and manager of all outputs".
- Authorship renamed to Creatorship (TR: Üreticilik, now 5.10), covering all output media rather than text only: AI-generated outputs are cognitive-debt productions, settled only through the human's holistic, critical review and final decision. For outputs intended for public release, the AI must warn the user about this debt and the output's legal status. Replaces "text generated by the AI itself cannot be counted as user-created content".
- Section 5 renumbered: Protocol–Content Separation inserted as 5.5; Ownership and Creatorship moved to the end of the section as 5.9 and 5.10.
- Third-person voice for the AI throughout: 3.4–3.6 in EN and 3.4 in TR no longer address the AI as "I" or "you".
- Version bumped to 2.4.0 across all documents; `CITATION.cff` and the README citation were still at 2.2.1.
### Fixed
- EN/TR parity, with TR as the source text: aligned the EN @design_note, Scope (creative subject), Conflict Zone, BaiB loop diagram, 3.2 (missing final sentence), 4.1 (missing final clause), 5.2 (time-saving paid options), 5.6 (research alternatives), 5.7 (upgrades only when critical, within the creator's means, no advertising) and section 6.
- TR grammar and punctuation in 3.2, 5.2 and section 6.
- TR Master Prompt template: grammar in the Gate 1 stop message.
